## Verdict (would I sign up?)
**Probably yes, but warily.** The signup → profile → listing → apply funnel is genuinely smooth, the commission is shown clearly *before* I commit, privacy is reassuring, and the availability grid is teacher-friendly. What gives me pause: the job board looks like **filler/test data** (215 jobs, almost all the identical "Primary 5 · Mathematics (PSLE), S$30–60/hr, Singapore, 2×/wk" card), and the **subject↔level taxonomy lets me build nonsense combinations** (A-Math for Primary 1 / Kindergarten), which makes a curriculum-literate teacher question how well the platform "knows" SG education. Nothing blocked money or leaked personal information.

## ✓ Worked well / built trust
- **Commission shown before commit, with a worked example.** Signup step 3 (/register/commission) states "50% of the first month… charged once, after your first confirmed session. You keep 100% of every month after that," with a concrete S$60/hr × 4 × 1.5h = S$360 → S$180 calc, and the **"Create account" button is correctly disabled until I tick the acknowledgement.** Fair and transparent. (moe-03-commission png)
- **Availability grid is genuinely teacher-friendly.** Day × time grid (08:00–22:00), 1-hour/2-hour block toggle, and quick-set shortcuts ("Weekday evenings", "Weekend afternoons", "Clear"). "Weekday evenings" did exactly what I'd want in one tap. (moe-04-availability-grid png)
- **Listing wizard groups levels sensibly** (Pre-School / Primary / Secondary (O-Level) / JC (A-Level) / IB / Poly-Uni / Adult) — much clearer than the flat list on the profile page. Strong mother-tongue + Humanities + IB/Poly/Uni coverage in the subject list.
- **Profile-completeness gate is explicit** — the dashboard tells me exactly what's missing ("a public handle") rather than failing silently (despite the MOE-3 contradiction).
- **Apply flow is clear and confirmed** — modal explains "Applying does not book the job," lets me add a pitch, and on success the button flips to "✓ Interest sent" with a "View in My Applications" link. My Pipeline shows status "Applied" + my pitch + a **Withdraw** button. No silent failure.
- **Privacy messaging is consistent and reassuring** — "Contact details are never shown / never shared," admin-coordinated introductions, repeated on dashboard, job detail, interview and match tabs. As a teacher this is exactly the protection I want.
- **Subject filter on /assignments works** (215 → 11 when I picked A-Math (O-Level)).
- **Mobile @390px is clean** — no horizontal overflow on /assignments or /tutor/profile (measured scroll Width  equals  client Width  equals  390); hamburger nav, filters and chips stack and wrap correctly. (moe-06, moe-07)

## Console / errors
- No 500s encountered. Only console error was an expected pre-login 401 GET auth me interface operation on public pages. Profile/listing/apply pages were error-clean.
