# Cover concept

Historical design asset, converted from documentation HTML on 18 October 2026. The source supplied no approval date. This file preserves its wording and visual intent; it does not certify the claims in that wording.

## Text on the cover

- Brand: InTuition SG.
- Category: Tuition Marketplace.
- Star treatment: five stars followed by "curated, verified tutors".
- Headline: "Where Singapore finds its tutors, matched with care."
- Supporting line: "Curated matching, scheduled interviews, and reviews you can trust."
- Three feature labels: "Verified tutors", "Curated matches", "Interview & review".
- Footer domain label: the proposed product domain.
- Footer action line: "Post a request · Get matched".
- Diagram labels: TUTEE and TUTOR.

## Visual intent

The source arranged the brand and marketplace label across the top, a large editorial headline and supporting text at the left, and a tutor/tutee relationship illustration at the right. The illustration connects two participant circles through a central match spark, with small stars as decoration. The brand mark combines an open book and a spark. The footer contains the domain and action line.

The palette uses deep navy, a warm paper background, clay accents and muted gold. Serif emphasis in the headline contrasts with the simpler supporting text. This is a written design reference; the original layout/CSS/SVG instructions are not retained as executable code.

## Claim boundary

The stars and verified-tutor wording are historical concept copy. They do not establish reviews, verification evidence, a live service or approval of a universal badge. The current requirements retain O05 for the meaning/evidence of that badge and O11 for approved testimonial content. Keep any eventual public claims tied to real evidence and the current requirements.
